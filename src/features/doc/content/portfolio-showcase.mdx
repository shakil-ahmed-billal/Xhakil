Efficiency is at the core of my work style. I’m a fan of tools that stay out of the way but provide immense power. The **Global Command Palette** is my tribute to that mindset. To build a portfolio that reflects the future, I used the tools of the future:

- **Next.js 16 (App Router)** for blazing-fast speed.
- **Tailwind CSS v4** for a design system that’s ahead of its time.
- **TypeScript** for the reliability that professional engineering demands.
- **Framer Motion** for micro-animations that make the interface feel "alive."
